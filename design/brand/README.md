# Bali Closer logo system

## Logo architecture

- Universal mark: a rising sun above two contour lines inspired by Bali rice terraces.
- Russian horizontal wordmark: `BALI · БЛИЖЕ`.
- English horizontal wordmark: `BALI · CLOSER`.
- Staff uniform back: stacked mark with `BALI CLOSER`.
- Typeface: Manrope ExtraBold, with Arial as a production fallback. Convert wordmarks to outlines before sending artwork to a printer that cannot embed Manrope.

## Official single-colour versions

- Champagne gold: `#E8C88E` for deep forest backgrounds.
- White: `#FFFFFF` for dark photography or very dark backgrounds.
- Deep forest: `#071510` for light backgrounds and documents.

The SVG masters use champagne gold. Because every logo is single-colour, vendors may replace all gold strokes and fills with either official white or deep forest without changing opacity, stroke width, spacing, or proportions.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: at least the height of the sun semicircle on every side.
- Horizontal minimum width: 150 px on screen or 40 mm in print.
- Stacked minimum width: 32 mm in print.
- Symbol minimum size: 24 px on screen or 8 mm in print.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, use gradients, or rearrange the rays and contour lines.

## Uniform recommendation

- Shirt colour: deep forest `#071510`.
- Front: hero slogan only, two-colour print.
- Back: `logo-stacked-en-gold.svg`, centred 7 cm below the collar, recommended print width 90 mm.
