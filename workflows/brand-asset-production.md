# Brand asset production workflow

## Goal

Keep the Bali Closer identity consistent whenever a new website surface, uniform, or vendor deliverable is produced.

## Trigger

Run this workflow whenever a new branded medium is requested or an existing logo placement is changed.

## Source of truth

- Universal symbol: simple half sunrise with eleven straight rays and one horizontal baseline; no contour or wave lines.
- Website wordmarks: `BALI · БЛИЖЕ` for Russian and `BALI · CLOSER` for English.
- Staff uniform back: stacked symbol plus `BALI CLOSER`.
- Official colours and size rules: `design/brand/README.md`.

## Workflow

1. Identify the medium, physical or digital dimensions, background colour, and reproduction method.
2. Select horizontal, stacked, or symbol-only composition according to available space.
3. Select one official single-colour treatment with sufficient contrast.
4. Prepare a preview at actual placement proportions.
5. For physical production, convert wordmarks to vector outlines and include dimensions and ink colours.
6. Present one decision-ready brief linking the preview and production assets.
7. Obtain a checkpoint approval before publishing or mass production.
8. For Russian customer-facing placements, obtain fluent Russian review before publication.

## Definition of done

- Correct locale-specific wordmark is used.
- Minimum size and clear space rules pass.
- Digital assets work at desktop and mobile widths.
- Vendor files contain no missing fonts and specify physical print dimensions.
- Final approval and, where applicable, Russian-language review are recorded.
